My goal is to help build products that solve real problems in **energy, the environment and bureaucracy**.

## 🔭 Featured projects

**[payroll-sentinel](https://github.com/DanielznRtm/payroll-sentinel)**: validates payroll data before it enters a salary benchmark, flagging each row as *accept*, *review* or *block* with a reason. It combines Brazilian labor-law rules, three voting anomaly detectors (robust z-score, IQR, Isolation Forest) and a guard-railed LLM layer. 104 tests, CI, synthetic data calibrated on RAIS 2025.
[Live demo (Streamlit)](https://payroll-sentinel.streamlit.app) · [In-browser demo, your data never leaves your computer (Hugging Face)](https://huggingface.co/spaces/Danielrtm1/payroll-sentinel)

**[Bixonet](https://github.com/DanielznRtm/Bixonet)**: helps first-year students who move to a new city find housing near their campus (student houses, studios and official university contacts), with a distance-ranked list and a map. Built with Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase/PostGIS and MapLibre; tested with Vitest, pgTAP and Playwright.

**RainCat** *(in progress, Global Sustainability Challenge 2026-27)*: low-cost ESP32 sensor nodes with on-device inference for finer-grained flood forecasting in small cities.
